# Aurator

Aurator turns your speech into clean text in whatever app you are using, on your Mac, on device. Your voice and your words stay with you.

## Download

Download the latest version here.

[Download Aurator](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ndmr0/aurator/main/Aurator.dmg)

Open the DMG and drag Aurator into your Applications folder. The app is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens without a warning. It updates itself from then on.

## Requirements

macOS 26 or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.

## Updates

Aurator checks for updates on its own and installs them when you approve. You can also check any time from the menu bar icon, Check for Updates.

## Privacy

Speech to text and cleanup run on your Mac. Your recordings and transcripts are stored locally, and you can back them up or remove them from Settings.
